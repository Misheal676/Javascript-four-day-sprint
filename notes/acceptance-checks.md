\# Focus Board: Acceptance Checks



Mark each one PASS or FAIL after testing in the browser.



\## Tasks

1\. \[ ] Add a task with a title and priority. It appears in the list.

2\. \[ ] Submit a blank title. An error shows and no task is added.

3\. \[ ] Submit a title over 80 characters. An error shows.

4\. \[ ] Add "Write tests", then " write tests ". A duplicate error shows.

5\. \[ ] Tick a task. It shows as completed. Untick it. It is open again.

6\. \[ ] Edit/rename a task. The new title shows and is saved.

7\. \[ ] Delete a task. It is removed (with confirmation or undo).



\## Filters, search, stats

8\. \[ ] Click Completed. Only completed tasks show. Open and All work too.

9\. \[ ] Search "WRITE" (capitals). Matching tasks show.

10\. \[ ] The page shows total, completed, open counts and a completion percentage.



\## Persistence

11\. \[ ] Add a task, reload. It is still there.

12\. \[ ] Store "not-json" under the app key, reload. The app still works and shows a notice.



\## Suggestions and offline

13\. \[ ] Suggestions load. Clicking one fills the title box only.

14\. \[ ] Offline: error and Retry appear, and tasks still work.



\## Extras

15\. \[ ] Undo restores the most recently deleted task.

16\. \[ ] Export downloads JSON. Import validates it before replacing data.
